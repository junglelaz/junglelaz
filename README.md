## How I work

I bring creative direction and product design into technical prototyping and iterative development. My practice includes collaboration with specialist contributors and AI-assisted tools for implementation, exploration and documentation. These projects demonstrate the resulting experiences and decisions; they should not be read as claims that I personally hand-wrote every line of code.

Project READMEs describe each public package's scope, dependencies, credits and limitations. MIDI Commons explicitly documents AI-assisted composition and validation. Third-party assets and upstream work remain credited to their respective creators.
